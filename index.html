MPSC OMR checker

Upload the answer key and a photo or PDF of the filled OMR sheet. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Answer key

PDF or image of the key. If the key is split over several pages or photos, select them all together or add them one after another. You can also paste rows below: either question number plus series A, B, C, D, or one answer per question (1.B, 2 C, or a plain string like ABCDDACB). Use # for a cancelled question.

2. OMR sheet

Keep the whole sheet in the frame, including all 4 black corner dots. Use even light and a flat surface.
